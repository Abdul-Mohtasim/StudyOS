Frequently Asked Questions

Using StudyOS

What is a streak?

Your streak is the number of days in a row you've hit your daily study goal. It resets to zero if a full day passes with no finished focus session or completed task, so try to log at least a little progress every day.

If you know you'll miss a day, you can protect your streak with a Streak Freeze from the Gem Shop.

How do daily goals work?

You pick a daily study-time goal in Settings → Preferences. Your ring on the Today screen fills up as you complete focus sessions and tasks, and turns green once you reach 100%.

What are gems and how do I earn them?

Gems are earned by finishing focus sessions, completing tasks and keeping your streak alive. Spend them in the Gem Shop on Streak Freezes, Double Gems boosts, extra themes and gifts for friends.

How does the Leaderboard work?

The Leaderboard on the Friends tab ranks you and the people you follow by gems earned. Follow a classmate to see them appear there, and invite a friend to StudyOS to earn a bonus for both of you.

Can I use StudyOS offline?

Yes. Tasks, notes and focus sessions keep working without a connection, and everything syncs to your account the next time you're online. You can check sync status under Settings → Sync and offline.

Account Management

How do I change my name, username or email?

Go to Settings → Profile to update your display name and photo. Your email is tied to your sign-in method; if you signed up with email and password, you can change it from Settings → Account → Profile.

How do I find and follow friends on StudyOS?

Open the Friends tab and tap the add icon to search by username, share your QR code, or invite contacts. You can follow, unfollow or block someone from their profile page.

How do I export or reset my data?

Settings → Export my data lets you download a copy of your tasks, notes and progress. There's no one-tap full reset yet — email us from the Feedback form below if you need your study history cleared.

I'm having trouble accessing my account.

If you signed in with Google, use the "Continue with Google" button with the same Google account. If you use email and password, try resetting your password from the login screen. Still stuck? Send us a message below.

How do I delete my account and my data?

Email us from the address linked to your account with the subject "Delete my StudyOS account" and we'll delete your profile, tasks, notes, planner and focus-session history within 30 days. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Gems & Shop

What can I buy in the Gem Shop?

The Gem Shop offers Streak Freezes (holds up to 2), a Double Gems boost for 24 hours, extra app themes, and gifting gems to a friend. Everything is paid for with gems you earn by studying — no real money involved.

Is StudyOS free?

Yes, StudyOS is completely free to use. The Gem Shop only accepts gems earned in-app, and there are no subscriptions, ads or in-app purchases with real money.

I didn't receive the gems I spent on something.

Gem Shop purchases are checked and recorded on our server, so a flaky connection can occasionally cause a delay. Try reopening the app; if the item still hasn't appeared after a few minutes, send us the details below.
