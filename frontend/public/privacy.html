StudyOS Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

StudyOS ("we", "our", "the app") is a study planner with tasks, notes, a planner and a focus timer, available as a website and as an Android app. This policy explains what information StudyOS collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Google user data (Sign in with Google). If you choose to sign in with Google, StudyOS accesses only the basic profile information Google provides: your name, email address and profile picture, together with a Google ID token used to verify your identity. StudyOS does not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, contacts or any other Google data.

Account information. If you sign up with email and password instead, we collect your name, email address and a password, which we store only in hashed (unreadable) form.

Content you create. We store the tasks, subjects, notes, planner entries, focus-session records (start time and duration) and settings (such as daily goal and theme) that you enter, so they are available when you sign in again on any device.

On your device. The app stores a sign-in token and a few preferences (theme, daily goal, hidden subjects) in your device's or browser's local storage so you stay signed in and your preferences are remembered.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

2. How we use information

  • Name, email and profile picture: to create your account, identify you, show your name and picture inside the app, and respond to your messages.
  • Google ID token: used once at sign-in to verify who you are with our server; we do not use it to access any other Google service.
  • Tasks, notes, planner, focus sessions and settings: only to save and display them to you inside StudyOS.
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems and maintain the app.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models.

3. Sharing and disclosure

We do not share your data with other people or companies, except: (a) Google, which provides the sign-in service; (b) the hosting and database providers that store your data on our behalf and are only permitted to process it to run StudyOS; and (c) when required by law. Other users of StudyOS cannot see your tasks, notes or other content.

StudyOS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing us at abdulmohtasim034@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account with the subject "Delete my StudyOS account". We will delete your account and all associated data (profile, tasks, notes, planner, focus sessions and settings) within 30 days. You can also remove StudyOS's access from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5. Security

Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and passwords are stored hashed. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children

StudyOS is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above will change when we do.
